# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Readers interested in DevOps, cloud, and software engineering, plus people who want to learn about and connect with Youssef El Jirari.

## Product Purpose
Use Youssef El Jirari as the site identity and eljirari.me as the canonical domain.
A personal engineering blog presenting Youssef and his writing. Preserve the existing article and its URL while giving the website a sleek, modern visual refresh.

## Brand Personality
Curious, personable, grounded. The user approved a spacious layout, warm neutral colors, orange accents, and a portrait as the focal point.

## Design Principles
- Put the person and the writing first.
- Keep navigation and reading straightforward.
- Retain authentic content and publication dates.
- Make the design work on small and large screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Use semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, descriptive image alternatives, readable contrast, and reduced-motion support. No specialized accessibility requirements were supplied.
